Our Journey

Milestones That Defined Us

Each chapter of the Aurelia story reflects a bold step forward in luxury hospitality and landmark real estate development.

1999

The Foundation

Alexander Harrington and Victoria Chen co-found Aurelia Estates with a singular vision: to create hospitality experiences defined by quiet luxury and architectural integrity. The first property, a restored Georgian townhouse in Mayfair, opens its doors to just twelve guests.

2005

First Landmark Resort

Aurelia opens its first full-service five-star resort on the Mediterranean coast. The 80-villa property earns a Forbes Five-Star rating within its first year and establishes the brand’s signature blend of local culture and international luxury standards.

2012

$50M Strategic Expansion

Following a landmark $50M capital raise, Aurelia acquires three premium sites across Dubai, Singapore, and Monaco. The group’s development pipeline expands to include mixed-use commercial landmarks alongside hospitality assets.

2019

Global Brand Recognition

Aurelia Estates is recognised by Condé Nast Traveler and Robb Report as one of the world’s leading luxury hospitality groups. The portfolio surpasses 500,000 guests hosted across five continents, with a 98% satisfaction rate.

2026

$200M+ Portfolio & Beyond

Today, Aurelia manages a portfolio exceeding $200M in landmark projects across hospitality, branded residences, and commercial developments. Two new flagship properties are under construction in London and the French Riviera, with plans to enter the North American market by 2028.

“Our first twenty-five years were about building a legacy of excellence. The next twenty-five will be about redefining what luxury hospitality can achieve—where every property tells a story, and every guest becomes part of ours.”

Alexander Harrington & Victoria Chen
